Atlas Process
Six stages · walk to sign-off

How a job actually runs.

No magic, no surprises. Every Atlas job follows the same six stages, whether it's a 12-yard garage slab or a 400-yard commercial foundation. Here's what happens, when it happens, and what you get out of each stage.

Stage 01

Site Walk

1–2 hrs · on-site

We meet at the job, walk it with you, and read the drawings. We flag anything that'll bite later: access for the pump truck, drainage, soils surprises, existing rebar tie-ins, neighbor constraints. For residential, this is usually a lead estimator plus the crew foreman who'd run the job.

You get

Site notes Access plan Red flags, if any
Stage 02

Flat-Rate Bid

3–5 business days

One number, broken down by scope: formwork, rebar, mix, pump, labor, finish, demob. No hourly gotchas, no open allowances for things we could have figured out up front. If the drawings have gaps, we list the assumptions we bid to.

You get

Itemized bid (PDF) Scope assumptions Proposed start window
Stage 03

Schedule & Mix

1–2 weeks before pour

Signed contract, we slot you onto the pour calendar with a hard start date. We submit mix design to the engineer, coordinate the pump, order rebar, and file for inspection with the jurisdiction. You get a single thread with your lead and the office.

You get

Pour date · hard Mix design approval Inspection schedule
Stage 04

Form & Pour

2 days – 3 weeks

In-house carpenters set the forms, tie rebar, and dry-fit anything cast-in. Pre-pour inspection, mockup panel if the finish requires one. Pour day starts before sunrise: first truck at 6 am, last truck gone by early afternoon, crew on finish through strip time.

You get

Pre-pour walk Mockup sign-off Cylinder breaks
Stage 05

Strip & Finish

3–7 days after pour

Forms come off on the schedule the mix requires — not before. Faces dressed, edges broken, joints cut, sack rubs applied where speced. If the finish doesn't match the mockup, we fix it. Punch list walked on-site with you and the GC.

You get

Finished surface Punch-list walk Honor-work list (if any)
Stage 06

Sign-Off & Demob

1 day

Cylinder breaks at 28 days, lab report emailed. Cores if speced. Form ties pulled, patches done, site swept, spoils hauled. Final invoice matches the bid, minus anything we credited; no surprises. You get a ready surface and a handshake, on the day we said.

You get

28-day break report Final invoice Warranty letter

02 Typical timeline

From first walk to stripped wall, most residential jobs run 4–6 weeks. Commercial runs 8–14.

The long pole is usually permitting, not us. If drawings are stamped and the jurisdiction is moving, we can be on-site within two weeks of a signed contract for most residential scopes. Commercial coordination — MEP sleeves, embeds, inspection rounds — typically adds two to four weeks before the first pour.

We'll give you a real schedule at Stage 02 with every milestone dated. If something slides, you'll know before it does.
