Service 03 / 04 · Flatwork

Driveways, patios, slabs on grade.

Flatwork is where your eye lives. A slab that's out of plane, a joint in the wrong place, a trowel line that skips — you'll see it every day for the next forty years. We screed laser-flat, trowel by hand on the edges, and put the control joints where the building wants them, not where it's convenient.

Bid this scope

Scope

Driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, garage slabs, slab-on-grade (SOG), interior finished slabs, warehouse floors, architectural flatwork. Tolerances from broom-standard to F-min 50 polished floor.

Tools

Laser screed for anything over 1,000 sf. Ride-on power trowels for production. Hand trowels and walking edgers for borders, radii, and any pour where the eye is on it. Control joints cut early, not chiseled later.

Finishes we do often

Broom, salt, exposed aggregate, sand-wash, trowel, steel-trowel, burnished, polished, acid-stained. Each finish has a preferred mix and a preferred joint pattern — we'll walk you through the options at bid.
