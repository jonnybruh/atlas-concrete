Service 04 / 04 · Finishes

Board-form, sack rub, polish.

Architectural concrete is cast-in-place with the finish treated as the primary design element. The wall isn't painted or clad — what comes out of the forms is what you live with. That puts a lot of weight on mix, forms, and crew. We do mockup panels for every architectural finish before production. Every time.

01Finish library

Finish 01 /

Board-Form

Wood-grain texture pulled from milled boards used as form liner. Cedar is our default; Douglas fir, redwood, and reclaimed options on request. Grain can be read across an entire wall when boards are sequenced and reused properly.

Finish 02 /

Sack Rub

Slurry coat hand-applied to a stripped wall to fill tie holes and even out minor surface variation. Ranges from light (tie holes only) to full (uniform tone across the face). Skilled work — we do it ourselves.

Finish 03 /

Exposed Aggregate

Surface retarder on the form face, washed after strip to reveal the stone. Aggregate visual depends on mix design — we'll sample three mix options with different stones before production.

Finish 04 /

Polished Slab

Diamond-ground floors at 200, 400, 800, or 1500 grit. Densifier and sealer applied between passes. Works over most SOG mixes; best results over mixes designed for polish from day one.

Finish 05 /

Acid Stain

Reactive metallic stain that etches and colors the surface in one step. Variegated and organic — no two slabs look alike. Sealed with a penetrating sealer, never topical.

Finish 06 /

Chamfers & Reveals

Sharp-edge returns, rustication strips, cast-in reveal lines. Details we read off the drawings and build into the formwork. Easy to screw up, easy to get right if you take your time.

02The mockup process

A mockup panel is the cheapest insurance on a production pour. Build it first, pour it second.

  • 001Build the panel4 × 4 ft panel using the exact forms, ties, mix, and finish planned for production. Built in our yard or on-site.
  • 002Pour & stripPour follows the same mix design and crew that will run the real wall. Strip at the same age.
  • 003Review with architectArchitect and owner sign off on texture, tone, tie pattern, chamfer return, and sack rub intensity — or we adjust.
  • 004Lock the recipeMix, forms, and crew sequence are locked against the approved panel. That's the standard the real wall has to meet.
