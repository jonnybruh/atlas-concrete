03 How a Job Runs 06 stages

Stage 01 /

Site Walk

We meet on-site, read the drawings, and flag anything that'll bite later — access, drainage, soils, rebar tie-ins.

Stage 02 /

Flat-Rate Bid

One number, broken down by scope. No hourly gotchas. Turnaround is five business days for most jobs.

Stage 03 /

Schedule

We slot you on the pour calendar with a hard start date. You get the mix design and inspection plan up front.

Stage 04 /

Form & Pour

In-house carpenters set the forms. The day of the pour we arrive at 5:30, tools on the truck by 2.

Stage 05 /

Strip & Finish

Forms come off, faces are dressed, edges are broken, joints are cut. Punch list reviewed on-site with the GC.

Stage 06 /

Sign-Off

Cylinder breaks logged. Core samples if speced. A clean demob and a handshake, on the day we said.
